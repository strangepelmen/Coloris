How the engine works

1

Upload

Image is loaded onto a hidden canvas at full resolution for pixel‑accurate analysis.

MMCQ algorithm bins every pixel into 32³ color cubes, then finds the 5 most populous clusters.

MMCQ · O(n)

Each palette color is converted to CIE L*a*b* and compared against 202 database images using CIEDE2000.

CIEDE2000 ΔE

Frequently asked

The app loads your image onto a hidden HTML canvas, reads every pixel's RGB value, then uses the Modified Median Cut Quantization (MMCQ) algorithm to find the most representative colors. Each pixel is binned into a 32×32×32 color cube, and the most populous cubes are averaged to produce the final palette.
